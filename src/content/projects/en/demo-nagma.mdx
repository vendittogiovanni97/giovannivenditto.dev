# Nagma Tattoo CRM — Enterprise Tattoo Studio Management

## Project Overview
**Nagma Tattoo CRM** is an enterprise management platform (CRM) designed for the complete operational and financial management of tattoo and piercing studios. The solution brings together a Node.js 22+ & Express 5 REST API with **Prisma 7.8 / PostgreSQL 17** and a modern **Next.js 16 (App Router)** and **Material-UI (MUI)** frontend.

---

## 1. The Challenge & Problem
Tattoo studios juggle complex scheduling, hygiene compliance, and financial splits typically managed on paper notebooks and scattered chats:
- **Frequent no-shows and scheduling overlaps**: An estimated 15% to 20% loss in monthly revenue due to missed appointments and artist station conflicts.
- **Legal liability on informed consent forms**: Obligation to preserve signed physical consent sheets for years, with recurring risk of damaged or lost health declarations.
- **Lack of financial clarity**: Substantial time spent calculating monthly payouts for resident and guest artists across varied percentage tiers and customer deposits.

---

## 2. Architecture & Engineering Solution

### 📅 Multi-Artist Interactive Calendar
- Advanced calendar view (FullCalendar & AG Grid) with artist color coding, time slot scheduling, and real-time appointment status tracking.
- Client profiles complete with health history/allergies, treatment timeline, and high-resolution artwork attachments.

### 💰 Automated PDF Consent Forms & Accounting
- Financial tracking of revenues, expenses, down payments, and automated split calculations between studio and artists with visual trend graphs.
- Automated generation of regulatory-compliant PDF informed consent forms via **PDFKit** with digital signatures, plus data exports in **Excel (.xlsx)** and **CSV**.

### ⚙️ Monorepo & Docker Pipeline
- Monorepo architecture managed with npm workspaces, Docker Compose for PostgreSQL 17, and automated unit/integration test suites in Jest and Playwright.

---

## 3. Business Results & ROI
The rollout of Nagma CRM delivered transformational operational efficiency:
- **-65% customer no-shows**: Transparent scheduling and automated booking workflows minimized empty studio slots.
- **100% elimination of paper archives**: Thousands of signed legal consents securely digitized and instantly retrievable.
- **Real-time financial visibility**: Eliminated hours of manual reconciliation at month-end through automated revenue sharing.
