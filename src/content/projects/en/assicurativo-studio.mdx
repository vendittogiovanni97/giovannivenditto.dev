# Portale Assicurativo — Enterprise Management & OCR Pipeline

## Project Overview
**Portale Assicurativo** is an enterprise platform designed to digitalize the management of insurance clients, contracts, and expirations. The application combines an ultra-reactive frontend built with **React 19 + Vite + Material UI** with a Node.js/TypeScript backend and **MongoDB**.

---

## 1. The Challenge & Problem
Before the platform was introduced, policy management suffered from severe operational friction:
- **Time-consuming manual data entry**: Staff spent up to 15 minutes manually transcribing customer records, coverages, and premiums from multi-page PDF contracts.
- **Human error risk**: Frequent discrepancies in tax IDs, vehicle license plates, and premium figures that impacted insurance coverage and accounting.
- **Security & compliance exposure**: Lack of strict access control and auditing on sensitive client and health documents.

---

## 2. Architecture & Engineering Solution

### 🔍 Real-Time Watcher & OCR + LLM Pipeline
- **Real-Time Directory Monitoring**: Backend watches incoming document directories via *Chokidar watcher*.
- **Combined OCR + LLM Pipeline**: Uploaded PDF policy documents are parsed using a combined **OCR (Tesseract)** and **LLM (Gemini)** pipeline to automatically extract and structure customer data, limits, premiums, and expiration dates into database-ready JSON.

### 🛡️ Security Hardening
- **IDOR Protection & RBAC**: Role-based access control (`admin`, `super_admin`) with ownership verification on sensitive attachments and resources.
- **MIME Magic Bytes Verification**: Binary signature inspection for image and PDF uploads to prevent malicious file uploads.
- **Path Traversal Mitigation**: Controlled path resolution with strict system whitelisting.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard & AG Grid
- High-performance tabular data management with **AG Grid 32** supporting fast search, advanced filtering over 10,000+ records, and accounting report export.

---

## 3. Business Results & ROI
Deploying the platform delivered immediate, quantifiable business outcomes:
- **-80% manual processing time**: The intake and indexing time for a policy dropped from 15 minutes to under 20 seconds.
- **Zero data entry errors**: LLM extraction validated against regex rules eliminated transcription mistakes.
- **100% compliance and confidentiality**: Strict RBAC and zero unauthorized access to sensitive client files.
